# COS30008 — Data Structures & Patterns

A collection of past papers, important concepts, coding patterns and revision notes for **COS30008 (Data Structures & Patterns)** at Swinburne.

DSP can be a pretty challenging unit, especially when it comes to the final exam. I made this repo to put together the resources and concepts I found useful while preparing for it.

The goal isn't to cover the entire unit. It's to help students figure out what to focus on, understand the common coding patterns, and prepare a bit better for the exam.

## What's in here?

- **Past papers** — previous exam papers for practice and understanding the question formats.
- **Coding patterns** — copy/move constructors, trees, iterators, smart pointers and other recurring concepts.
- **Theory** — important questions, short explanations and revision notes.
- **Exam preparation** — what to focus on, common mistakes and tips for handwritten C++.
- **Practice** — examples and exercises to test your understanding.

## Where to start?

If you're preparing for the final exam, I'd suggest:

1. Look through the past papers to get familiar with the question formats.
2. Identify the coding patterns that keep appearing.
3. Practise writing the code yourself without looking at solutions.
4. Go through the theory questions and important concepts.
5. Try solving a full paper under exam conditions.

You'll probably notice similarities between some of the papers. Understanding those patterns can make the preparation a lot more manageable.
