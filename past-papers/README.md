## Things worth noticing

**2022 and January 2024:** These papers are particularly similar. Both focus heavily on ternary trees, copy/move semantics, prefix iterators and theory.

**2021:** Similar tree and copy/move concepts, but with a postfix iterator instead.

**2023:** A different structure, with more focus on smart pointers, linked lists, custom strings and bidirectional iterators.

A few concepts worth understanding across these papers:

- Copy constructors and copy assignment
- Move constructors and move assignment
- Trees, recursion and memory management
- Iterators and traversal
- Smart pointers
- Short theory questions

## How I'd practise

1. Open a paper and try identifying the concepts being tested.
2. Attempt the easier functions first.
3. Practise writing the harder functions without checking your notes.
4. Compare papers and look for similar patterns.
5. Attempt a complete paper under timed conditions.

The point isn't to memorise the papers. It's to understand the patterns well enough to handle different versions of the same problem.

**Note:** The 2021 paper includes student-written answers. Don't assume all of them are correct.

Past papers are for revision only. Future exams can be completely different, so don't rely on these as predictions.
